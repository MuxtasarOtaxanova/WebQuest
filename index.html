Our Changing Planet

A WebQuest for grades 8–9: discover why Earth is changing and what you can do about it.

1. Introduction

Glaciers are melting, seas are rising and summers are getting hotter. Scientists around the world agree: our planet is changing fast, and people are the main cause.

Your role: You are a Young Climate Expert invited to the school’s Green Summit. Click the cards to explore what is happening.

2. Task

Mission: Work in a group of 3–4. Research climate change and create a Climate Action Plan for your city (a poster, a short presentation or a one-page infographic).

Your plan must include:

  • 2 main causes of climate change
  • 3 effects on nature and people
  • 5 realistic solutions for your city or school
  • 1 personal pledge from every team member

🧠 Quick Quiz

6. Conclusion

Congratulations! You have learned that climate change is caused mostly by greenhouse gases from human activity, that it affects nature and people everywhere, and that small actions together make a big difference.

Think about it: What is one habit you will change this week?

7. Teacher’s Page

This page is for teachers. Enter the code to open it (demo code: teacher).

Learning objectives: students explain causes and effects of climate change, evaluate sources, propose solutions and work collaboratively.
Level and time: grades 8–9, CEFR A2–B1. Two or three 45-minute lessons.
Lesson plan:
  1. Lesson 1: Introduction, Task, group formation, research
  2. Lesson 2: Build the Climate Action Plan
  3. Lesson 3: Presentations, peer feedback, quiz
Tips: assign roles (researcher, designer, speaker, checker); remind students to use at least two different sources; check links before the lesson.
